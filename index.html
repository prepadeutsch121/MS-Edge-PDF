Features

Everything the form needs, nothing it doesn't.

Pick a PDF, fill in the details, and let MS Edge PDF generate the finished document — built for people who fill out the same kind of paperwork every day.

Load any PDF

Point the app at a source file with the "…" browse button — no drag-and-drop gymnastics required.

Fill in the details

Name, price, registration number — type straight into plain fields, no PDF form editor to fight with.

Process in one click

Hit "Process PDF" and the finished document is generated instantly — ready to save or hand off.

Stays on your PC

Everything is processed locally on your machine — no upload, no cloud round-trip for your files.

Lightweight window

One compact window with just the fields you need — nothing to configure, nothing to learn.

Built for Windows

Native look and feel on Windows 10 and 11, so it fits right in with the rest of your desktop.
